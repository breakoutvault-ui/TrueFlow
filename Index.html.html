📖 Breakout Setups — How This Works
🎯 What is this tab?
This tab finds stocks that have already made a big move and are now cooling down in a tight consolidation — waiting for the next breakout. Instead of chasing stocks AFTER they've broken out, this tab helps you spot them BEFORE the breakout happens.
📐 The 3 Patterns

🔥 EP (Episodic Pivot) — Stock gapped up 5%+ on 2x volume. Wait for pullback to gap area, then enter.

📐 VCP (Volatility Contraction) — 20%+ move then tight consolidation with declining volume. Buy on break above Pivot Level with volume.

🏴 HTF (High Tight Flag) — RARE. 80%+ surge then <25% pullback. Entry on break above pivot.

🔄 EMA Reclaim — Dipped below 9 EMA yesterday, bounced back today. Entry near 9 EMA level.
📊 Reading the Columns

Pivot — THE breakout price. ✅ ABOVE = confirmed. 🎯 = within 3% (watch closely).
Base % — Pullback depth. Under 15% = strong hands. Over 25% = avoid.
Contract — Consolidation tightness. Higher = better. 0.7+ excellent.
Vol Dry — Volume declining? ✅ = sellers exhausted. ❌ = still selling.
Base Days — 10-40 days ideal. Too short = unstable. Too long = fading.
Reclaim — Bounced back above 9 EMA after dip.
✅ Ideal Setup Checklist
☑️ Pattern: VCP or HTF   ☑️ Base%: Under 15%   ☑️ Contraction: 0.5+
☑️ Vol Dry: ✅ Yes   ☑️ Pivot: 🎯 Within 3%   ☑️ Category: A or AC
Entry: Close above Pivot with volume   Stop: Below base low

📖 Glossary
Grade: Auto-classification based on revenue growth, net profit growth, and operating margin trends.
Verdict: Compares result quality with recent price action — identifies if a stock's move is genuine or misleading.
Post-Res Move: Price change % since the result announcement date — shows how market reacted to the results.
OPM Δ: Operating Profit Margin change vs same quarter last year. Positive = margins improving.
Smart $: Accumulation = 2+ of Promoter/FII/MF increased holdings. Distribution = 2+ decreased.
New Inst: Stocks where FII/MF had zero or negligible holding previously but entered this quarter — fresh institutional interest signal.
Mom Cat: A = above all EMAs (strongest), B = below daily but above weekly (watch), C = fresh weekly crossover (early stage), AC = both A+C (best setup).
EMA Zone: IN = within 3% of 9 EMA (ideal entry), NEAR = 3-5%, FAR = >5% (extended, avoid fresh entry).
