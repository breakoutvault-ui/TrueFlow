What this screener is — and how to trade from it

One board for every US stock in the universe. Each row is a stock, each column a data point, each column header a multi-select filter. Instead of hopping between Scanner, EMA Signals and Breakout Setups, you start from a clean uptrend and keep adding conditions until only the best setups are left.
The daily routine: open ⭐ A+ Setups → glance at Price/Vol and RVOL to see price moving on real volume → check %9E for a low-risk entry → confirm Smart $ is not selling → hit 📋 TradingView to push your shortlist to a watchlist.

The setups — and how you'd trade each

The QM / Setup column tags the pattern a stock is in. These are the Qullamaggie-style continuation setups:
🔥 EP
Episodic Pivot — gapped up 5%+ on ~2× volume on a catalyst. Wait for a pullback toward the gap, then enter.
📐 VCP
Volatility Contraction — a big move, then a tight base with volume drying up. Buy the break above the pivot, on volume.
🚩 HTF
High Tight Flag — rare: an 80%+ surge then a shallow (<25%) pause. Enter on the break above the pivot.
↩ Reclaim
EMA Reclaim — dipped below the 9 EMA, then closed back above it (a shakeout recovery). Entry near the 9 EMA.
🎯 Near Pivot
Coiled within 3% of the buy trigger — the highest-priority watch. Set an alert at the pivot.
✅ Broken Out
Already triggered above the pivot. Manage, or wait for a re-test of the pivot.

Reading the two mini-charts

Last 5
The last 5 daily closes as arrows — green ↑ up, red ↓ down, oldest → newest. A one-glance read of direction.
Price / Vol
A 5-session picture. The line is price across those 5 days — green if it ended higher than it started, red if lower. The bars are each day's volume vs its average (green ≥1.5×, cyan ≥1×, grey <1×); the rightmost bar is today (solid), older days are faded.
What to look for: a rising green line with tall green bars stacked on the right = price climbing on expanding volume (the strongest picture). Rising line + short/grey bars = a move on weak volume (suspect). Falling line + tall bars = heavy selling. Falling line + small bars = a quiet, healthy pullback.

The volume picture — three different questions

RVOL
Is volume showing up today? Today's volume ÷ its 20-day average. 1.5×+ = real participation.
Vol Signal
What does today's move say? Accum up on heavy volume · Weak up on light volume · Distrib down on heavy volume · Pullback down on light volume.
BO Vol
Did the breakout have real volume? The best relative volume on the breakout day or within ±3 sessions — the badge says which (BO-day vs ±3d). High = trustworthy.
Timeframe — important: RVOL and Vol Signal describe today's session measured against the stock's 20-day average volume (not a multi-day accumulation line). BO Vol describes the breakout window. Everything else — EMA category, 52W state, Setup — is the stock's state as of the latest close. Smart $ is the exception: it reads recent deals over a trailing window. Hover any filter option and it now tells you its exact timeframe.

Where the stock is — trend & breakout cycle

EMA category
Where it is in its trend: A above daily 9+20 & weekly 9+20 (full uptrend) · B below daily 9, above weekly (pullback) · C fresh weekly cross (early turn) · A+C both (strongest).
52W State
Where it is in its breakout cycle: 🎯 BO Zone coiling within 15% below the 52W high · 🟦 Breakout Base broke out, holding within 15% · 🚀 52W Base BO running >15% past it.
%9E / %Wk9E
Your entry gauge — distance from the daily / weekly 9 EMA. Near 0% = sitting on the line = low-risk add; a big number = extended, wait for a pullback.
Consol Days
How long it has been basing. A longer base often precedes a bigger move.
ADR%
Average Daily Range % - the typical daily high-to-low move over ~20 sessions, shown as a percent so a $120 and a $3,000 stock compare fairly. Higher = the stock travels more each day = more tradeable. Under ~3% tends to be too slow for momentum; 5%+ is a strong mover. Use the ADR% filter to drop names that don't move enough.
vs Sec
Stock vs Sector - the stock's return minus its sector's median return, over 1D / 1W / 1M (toggle in the column header). Positive = the stock is leading its sector. Sort it to find the relative-strength leaders.
Sector bars
Three mini bars = the stock's sector performance over 1D / 1W / 1M (median of the sector's stocks). Green up, red down - a quick read on whether the sector is hot.
Consol Days / Base Days filters
Filter for stocks that have built a base for at least N sessions - longer bases often precede bigger resolutions.
Earnings: Result Date, Verdict, Pre/Post moves, Result RVOL, React (ADR)
Result dates come automatically from SEC filings for the whole momentum universe. Pre-5d = run-up into the result; Post-1d / Post-5d = the reaction and drift after; Result RVOL = peak volume around the result day (1.5x+ with a green move = real conviction); React (ADR) = the post move in the stock own daily-range units. Verdict is a plain-English read: Positive surprise (up on heavy volume), Anticipation (running up before results), Sell the news (ran up then fell), Drifting up (kept rising after), Quiet (little move). Use the Result In / Status / Verdict / RVOL / Reaction filters to find stocks moving on real results. Numbers fill in automatically as companies report from mid-July; everything is price/volume based, no manual uploads.

Smart money & results

Smart $
What large investors did in recent SEC insider filings (a trailing window): Loading Up › Accumulating › Nibbling = buying; Offloading = selling — a red flag for longs.
Results
The latest reported quarter, graded STRONG / MIXED / WEAK.

⭐ The A+ Setup — the ideal checklist

The ⭐ A+ Setups preset flags the highest-confluence names — every box ticked at once:
1. A VCP or HTF coil pattern — a tightening base, not random chop
2. EMA category A or A+C — a real uptrend
3. Base older than 5 days and shallower than 10% — mature and tight
4. Volume drying up — dried within the base and still quiet now (last-5 avg RVOL under 0.8×)
5. A 2×+ volume day in the last 20 sessions — proof of a prior demand thrust
6. 52W State is 🎯 BO Zone or 🟦 Breakout Base
7. ADR% ≥ 3 — a name that actually moves when it goes

What this tab finds

Big institutions accumulate quietly over days — their algos drip-feed orders so it rarely appears as a reported SEC insider filing (which is what the Smart Money tab catches). What it does leave behind is a rising volume footprint: each session trades more than the 20-day norm and the ratio keeps climbing. This tab surfaces those names so you spot accumulation before the breakout.

The date window

Today rank by today's volume vs its 20-day average (single-day shockers).
This Week 5-session buildup — the classic staircase. Default.
This Month ~20-session buildup — slower, structural accumulation.
Custom measure buildup over a chosen span.

Reading the columns

Last 5 price action — green ↑ closed up, red ↓ down, oldest → newest.
Vol Buildup each bar = that session's volume ÷ 20-day average; newest highlighted. Rising staircase = building up.
RVOL × latest session's volume vs 20-day average. 2.0× = twice the normal volume.
Trend ▲ Rising / ■ Flat / ▼ Fading — slope of the bars across the window.

Setup active breakout / Qullamaggie pattern (EP, VCP, HTF, Reclaim).

Smart $ smart-money tier from SEC insider filings.
Score RVOL×16 + slope×14 + (sustained>1.4× ? +12), capped 0–100.

How to use it

The best longs line up: rising volume + Cat A/A+C + a breakout setup + accumulating smart money. Use "Rising only" + RVOL ≥ 2× to cut noise, then scan the chips for confluence.

📖 Breakout Setups — How This Works
🎯 What is this tab?
This tab finds stocks that have already made a big move and are now cooling down in a tight consolidation — waiting for the next breakout. Instead of chasing stocks AFTER they've broken out, this tab helps you spot them BEFORE the breakout happens.
📐 The 3 Patterns

🔥 EP (Episodic Pivot) — Stock gapped up 5%+ on 2x volume. Wait for pullback to gap area, then enter.

📐 VCP (Volatility Contraction) — 20%+ move then tight consolidation with declining volume. Buy on break above Pivot Level with volume.

🏴 HTF (High Tight Flag) — RARE. 80%+ surge then <25% pullback. Entry on break above pivot.

🔄 EMA Reclaim — Dipped below 9 EMA yesterday, bounced back today. Entry near 9 EMA level.
📊 Reading the Columns

Pivot — THE breakout price. ✅ ABOVE = confirmed. 🎯 = within 3% (watch closely).
Base % — Pullback depth. Under 15% = strong hands. Over 25% = avoid.
Contract — Consolidation tightness. Higher = better. 0.7+ excellent.
Vol Dry — Volume declining? ✅ = sellers exhausted. ❌ = still selling.
Base Days — 10-40 days ideal. Too short = unstable. Too long = fading.
Reclaim — Bounced back above 9 EMA after dip.
✅ Ideal Setup Checklist
☑️ Pattern: VCP or HTF   ☑️ Base%: Under 15%   ☑️ Contraction: 0.5+
☑️ Vol Dry: ✅ Yes   ☑️ Pivot: 🎯 Within 3%   ☑️ Category: A or AC
Entry: Close above Pivot with volume   Stop: Below base low

🏦 Smart Money — How This Works
SEC insider filings (Form 4), every trading day. We keep real open-market insider buys & sells ($10K+), tag 🏛️ 10% owners vs ⭐ star insiders (CEO / CFO / President / Chair / 10% owners), and score each stock by whether insiders are accumulating or distributing over the last 30 days. Each stock is cross-referenced with your Momentum universe for sector, EMA category, % from 9 EMA, returns and more.
🌡️ Cooking Tiers (net selling always overrides)

🔥 LOADING UP — Net buying + a ⭐ star buying, or 2+ institutions with repeat purchases.
📈 ACCUMULATING — Net buying with multiple institutions or repeat buying.
👀 NIBBLING — Net buying, light/early single-institution interest.
⚪ QUIET — No meaningful net flow.
🧊 OFFLOADING — Net selling. Caution.
⭐ Stars = CEO / CFO / President / Chair & 10% owners — the insiders whose buying matters most. Score 0–100 weights stars heaviest, then institutions, repeat buying and net inflow; it is 0 whenever a stock is net-sold. Hover any column header, the score, a symbol, or a tier badge for details.

📋 Earnings & Episodic Pivots — How This Works

What this tab is for. When a US company reports its quarterly earnings, that result is a catalyst — a reason for the stock to move. This tab watches every earnings result across your US universe and tells you, in plain English, whether the stock is setting up for a tradable move. Everything here is automatic, pulled from official SEC-filed earnings dates and each stock's own price action. Nothing to upload.

The idea: a big move usually comes in two legs. A stock sitting quietly gets a strong earnings result, jumps, and rallies — that's Leg 1. If the catalyst is real and big money is accumulating, it then pauses near its highs, tightens up, and pushes higher again — that's Leg 2. This tab tracks each stock through that whole life so you can catch either entry.

⚡ Gapped
Just popped on the result. The move is fresh.
📈 Drifting
Still stepping up — Leg 1 alive. Buy small pullbacks.
🛏️ Basing
Resting near its highs after the pop.
🌀 Coiling
Tightening at the highs — Leg 2 forming. Watch closely.
🚀 Resumed
Broke out again — Leg 2 fired.
💧 Faded
Gave the gap back. Catalyst failed — avoid.

◆EP — the Delayed Episodic Pivot (the premium setup). This is the one worth hunting. A stock that was flat and neglected before earnings — nobody saw it coming — then pops hard on the result with heavy volume. Because the crowd wasn't already in, these have the cleanest room to run. When you see the ◆EP mark, the stock was quiet, then ignited. Pair it with insider buying on the Smart Money tab and you have real conviction.

The columns, simply:

EPS Surprise — did the company beat or miss what analysts expected? ▲ green = beat, ▼ red = miss. A big beat is a stronger catalyst.
Pre-run — how much the stock moved in the 5 days before the result. Near zero = neglected (good for Delayed EP). Already up a lot = the crowd front-ran it (more tired).
Gap (1d) — the immediate reaction the day after results.
Drift (5d) — how it continued over the next week (the drift that Leg 1 rides).
RVOL — result-day volume vs its normal. Above 1.5× means real conviction, not noise.
Read — the plain-English summary of what happened.

How to use it: Tap Delayed EP above to find fresh quiet-then-ignite names (Leg 1). Tap Leg 2 setups to find stocks coiling at their highs, ready to resume. Cross-check any name against the Smart Money tab — if insiders are buying while it bases, that's your highest-conviction picture.

🔄 Sector Rotation — How This Works
What is "sector rotation"?
At any given time the market's money tends to crowd into a few sectors (say Banks or Defence) and leave others. As those leaders get tired, money rotates into the next strong sector. Catching that shift early means you're buying stocks in a sector that's just starting to attract money — instead of chasing one that's already run.

Where does the data come from?
Nothing new to upload. This tab simply takes the stocks you already track in EMA Signals, Breakout Setups and Smart Money, groups them by sector, and adds up the picture for each sector. It refreshes on the same daily schedule as the rest of TrueFlow.
📊 The Rotation Score (0–100) — what goes into it
A single number telling you how strong a sector looks right now. Higher = stronger. It blends four things you already trust:

① EMA strength — 35%
How many stocks in the sector are bullish (trading above their daily 20 EMA). A sector where most stocks are above their EMAs is in an uptrend.

② Breakout activity — 15%
How many stocks are throwing up breakout setups, sharp moves or Qullamaggie patterns. Lots of breakouts = the sector is "hot".

③ Momentum & 1-week return — 25%
The sector's average momentum score and its average 1-week price gain.

④ Smart-money flow — 25%
Net institutional buying (SEC insider filings) into the sector over 30 days. Big money flowing in lifts the score; net selling drags it down.
What does the Trend arrow mean? (▲ IN / ▼ OUT / →)
The score tells you how strong a sector is; the arrow tells you which direction it's heading. It compares the recent pace (last 7 days of smart-money flow, last 1 week of returns) against the medium term. ▲ IN = money is accelerating into the sector (rotating in — the early signal you want). ▼ OUT = money is leaving. → = steady.

The three views
• 🔥 Sector Scorecard — every sector ranked best-to-worst. This is your daily health-check of the market. Hover any number for its meaning; click a row to drill in.
• 🔄 Rotation Radar — only the sectors that are actively rotating IN or OUT, side by side. Use it to catch a move early.
• 🔍 Sector Detail — pick one sector and see its leading stocks, the names smart money is loading, and the laggards. Three buttons jump you straight into EMA Signals / Breakout / Smart Money already filtered to that sector.

How to actually use it
① Open the Scorecard each morning — note the top 3–4 sectors. ② Check the Radar — is anything new rotating IN? ③ Click into a strong sector, see its leaders, and hit "→ EMA Signals" to find the exact stock near its 9 EMA to trade. You'll also notice a small 🔄 score-badge next to each stock's sector inside the EMA & Breakout tabs — so you always know if a setup is in a strong or weak sector.
